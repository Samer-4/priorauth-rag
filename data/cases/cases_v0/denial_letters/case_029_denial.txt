## II. BASIS FOR DENIAL

**Primary Denial Reason: Off-Label Use / Failure to Meet Coverage Criteria Under Clinical Policy Bulletin CPB-0580**

This determination is made pursuant to **Humana Clinical Policy Bulletin CPB-0580: Glucagon-Like Peptide-1 (GLP-1) Receptor Agonists and Dual GIP/GLP-1 Agonists for Weight Management and Glycemic Control**, effective January 1, 2025, and last revised March 14, 2025.

Under CPB-0580, coverage of semaglutide 2.4 mg (Wegovy®) for chronic weight management requires that **all** of the following criteria be satisfied:

1. The member has a documented Body Mass Index (BMI) of **≥ 30 kg/m²**; **OR** a BMI of **≥ 27 kg/m²** in the presence of at least one weight-related comorbidity (e.g., type 2 diabetes mellitus, hypertension, obstructive sleep apnea, or dyslipidemia managed as a direct sequela of excess weight);

2. The member has documented failure of a structured, comprehensive weight management program (i.e., dietary modification, behavioral counseling, and increased physical activity) of **at least six (6) consecutive months** duration, with supporting documentation submitted;

3. Semaglutide 2.4 mg is prescribed and monitored by, or in documented coordination with, a physician trained in obesity medicine, endocrinology, or a related specialty with expertise in weight management; and

4. The requested use falls within the **FDA-approved labeling** for semaglutide 2.4 mg (Wegovy®), or compelling peer-reviewed clinical evidence supports coverage of an off-label application under Humana's Off-Label Drug Use policy
