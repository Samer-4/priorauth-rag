## POLICY BASIS

This determination was made pursuant to:

**Cigna Clinical Policy Bulletin No. 0580**
*Weight Management Medications and Obesity Pharmacotherapy*
Effective Date: January 1, 2025 | Last Reviewed: March 15, 2025

This policy governs coverage of GLP-1 receptor agonists and GIP/GLP-1 dual agonists, including tirzepatide (Zepbound), when prescribed for chronic weight management. Coverage under CPB 0580 requires satisfaction of **all** applicable criteria as outlined in Section III.B (Tirzepatide – Zepbound), including but not limited to:

- Documentation of a Body Mass Index (BMI) ≥ 30 kg/m², **OR** BMI ≥ 27 kg/m² with at least one qualifying weight-related comorbidity as enumerated in the policy; **AND**
- Documentation that the member has participated in and demonstrated an inadequate response to a **structured, supervised behavioral weight management program** (minimum 6 months duration) encompassing dietary counseling, physical activity intervention, and behavioral modification strategies, administered or supervised by a qualified healthcare professional; **AND**
- Documentation that **at least one prior FDA-approved weight loss pharmacotherapy agent** (e.g., orlistat, phentermine-topiramate, bupropion-naltrexone) has been trialed at therapeutic doses for a minimum of 12 weeks and has resulted in less than 5% total body weight loss, or has been discontinued due to documented intolerance or contraindication; **AND**
- The medication is prescribed or co-managed by, or in documented consultation with, a physician specializing in **endocrinology, obesity medicine, or internal medicine/primary care** with documented expertise in obesity pharmacotherapy. Prescriptions originating solely from subspecialty practices not primarily engaged in obesity management (including cardiology) must include documentation of coordination with a qualifying provider.
