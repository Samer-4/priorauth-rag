### BASIS FOR DENIAL

#### 1. Off-Label Use — Not a Cigna-Covered Indication for This Agent

Ozempic® (semaglutide injection) is approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for the following indications only:

- **Adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in adults with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus**
- **Reduction of risk of major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) in adults with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and established cardiovascular disease**

The submitted request seeks authorization for semaglutide (Ozempic®) for the treatment of **non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD)** in a patient **without a confirmed diagnosis of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus**. This constitutes use of the medication for an indication that is **not approved by the FDA** for this specific agent and formulation and is therefore considered **off-label**.

Cigna's coverage policies do not provide benefits for off-label drug use unless the use is supported by sufficient clinical evidence published in peer-reviewed compendia or literature, and the use is consistent with Cigna's applicable Clinical Policy Bulletins. Based on current evidence review, this use does not meet that standard.
