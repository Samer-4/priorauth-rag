## Policy and Benefit Plan Citation

The member's benefit plan — **UnitedHealthcare Choice Plus, Group Benefit Plan Document effective January 1, 2025** — contains an explicit exclusion for prescription medications prescribed primarily or substantially for the purpose of weight loss or weight management, regardless of the presence of related comorbid conditions.

**Applicable Clinical Policy Bulletin:** UnitedHealthcare **Clinical Policy Bulletin No. 0580 — Weight Loss/Obesity Agents**, effective January 1, 2025 (supersedes prior version dated July 1, 2024).

**Relevant Plan Exclusion Language (Summary Plan Description, Section 7.4 — Prescription Drug Exclusions):**

> *"Benefits are not available for prescription drugs, biological products, or over-the-counter agents prescribed for, or primarily intended for, the purpose of weight reduction, weight loss maintenance, appetite suppression, or obesity management, including but not limited to glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonists and dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonists when prescribed for weight management indications, regardless of Body Mass Index (BMI) classification or the presence of associated comorbid conditions, unless such agents are otherwise specifically listed as a covered benefit under the member's prescription drug formulary and benefit schedule."*
