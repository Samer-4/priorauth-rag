**BASIS FOR DENIAL: INSUFFICIENT DOCUMENTATION**

The request for semaglutide (Ozempic®) has been denied because the clinical documentation submitted does not meet the required criteria established under CPB-0580 for approval of this medication at this time. Specifically, the following deficiencies were identified:

**1. Indication Not Supported for Requested Agent**

Semaglutide (Ozempic®) is FDA-approved and covered under this plan's pharmacy benefit as a **second-line or adjunctive agent for the treatment of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM)** in adults with inadequate glycemic control. The submitted documentation indicates a diagnosis of **prediabetes (ICD-10: R73.09)** and **obesity (ICD-10: E66.09)**, with a reported BMI of 33 kg/m². **A confirmed diagnosis of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus has not been established** in the submitted clinical record.

Per CPB-0580, Section III.A, coverage of Ozempic® requires documentation of a confirmed T2DM diagnosis, defined as one or more of the following: (a) fasting plasma glucose ≥ 126 mg/dL on two separate occasions; (b) hemoglobin A1c ≥ 6.5% on two separate occasions; or (c) two-hour plasma glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL during an oral glucose tolerance test. The submitted records include a single HbA1c value of 6.1%, which falls within the prediabetes range and does not satisfy diagnostic criteria for T2DM.
