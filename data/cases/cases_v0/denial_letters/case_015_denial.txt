## BASIS FOR DENIAL

**Reason for Denial: Benefit Plan Exclusion — Weight Loss/Obesity Agents**

Coverage for liraglutide 3.0 mg (Saxenda) is **denied** because the member's current benefit plan **explicitly excludes coverage for prescription drugs prescribed primarily for the purpose of weight loss or the treatment of obesity**, regardless of clinical indication, BMI, or the presence of obesity-related comorbidities.

This determination is made pursuant to:

> **Aetna Clinical Policy Bulletin No. 0580** — *Obesity: Medical and Surgical Treatments* (Effective Date: January 1, 2025; most recent review: March 15, 2025), which governs coverage criteria for pharmacological interventions for weight management.

> **Member Evidence of Coverage (EOC), Section 7.4 — Exclusions and Limitations:** *"Benefits are not available for drugs, devices, or programs used for the purpose of weight loss, including but not limited to anorectic agents, lipase inhibitors, GLP-1 receptor agonists prescribed for obesity management, and any medication whose primary indication as submitted is the reduction of body weight."*

Liraglutide 3.0 mg (Saxenda) is an FDA-approved glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist indicated as an adjunct to a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity for chronic weight management. The authorization request for **Sofia E. Alvarez (MRN-100248)** was submitted with a primary diagnosis of **morbid obesity (E66.01)**, confirming that the primary therapeutic intent of this prescription falls within the scope of the plan's exclusionary language.
