### POLICY BASIS FOR DENIAL

This determination is based upon **Aetna Clinical Policy Bulletin No. 0580: Glucagon-Like Peptide-1 (GLP-1) Receptor Agonists and Glucose-Dependent Insulinotropic Polypeptide (GIP)/GLP-1 Receptor Agonists for Weight Management**, effective January 1, 2025 (hereinafter "CPB 0580"), as well as the member's applicable plan documents and Evidence of Coverage.

Under CPB 0580, Section III(B)(2) — *Criteria for Approval of Tirzepatide (Zepbound) for Chronic Weight Management* — coverage of tirzepatide requires that the following conditions be met:

> *"The member must have documented, clinically supervised trials of at least two (2) formulary-preferred, lower-tier pharmacologic weight management agents, each trialed for a minimum of twelve (12) consecutive weeks at therapeutic doses, with documented inadequate response (defined as less than 5% total body weight loss) or documented intolerance precluding continued use, prior to consideration of tirzepatide."*

Additionally, CPB 0580, Section III(B)(1) requires:

> *"The member must have documentation of a structured, supervised lifestyle modification program (behavioral counseling, dietary intervention, and physical activity guidance) of no less than six (6) months duration within the twenty-four (24) months preceding the authorization request."*
