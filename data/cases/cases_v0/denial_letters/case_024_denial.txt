**Clinical Review Summary:**

The submitted clinical documentation for **Nicole B. Harrington (MRN-100257)** indicates the following:

- Age 44, female
- Body Mass Index (BMI): 36 kg/m²
- Diagnosed conditions: obesity (E66.01), right knee osteoarthritis (M17.11), prediabetes (R73.09)
- No documented prior trial of an alternative covered weight-management pharmacotherapy within the preceding 12 months
