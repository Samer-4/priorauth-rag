## BASIS FOR DENIAL

**Reason for Denial:** Insufficient Documentation to Support Medical Necessity

This request does not meet the criteria outlined in **Aetna Clinical Policy Bulletin No. 0580 — Obesity, Weight Management, and Anti-Obesity Drug Therapy (Revised: October 1, 2024)**, as well as the applicable provisions of your **Employer-Sponsored Group Health Benefit Plan's Prescription Drug Benefit Schedule (Specialty Tier — GLP-1/GIP Receptor Agonist Medications)**.

Tirzepatide (Zepbound) is a dual glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist indicated as an adjunct to a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity for chronic weight management. ### SPECIFIC DOCUMENTATION DEFICIENCIES

Upon review of the clinical information submitted with this request, Aetna has identified the following insufficient or missing documentation:

**1. Absence of Documented Prior Conventional Weight Loss Therapy**
Clinical Policy Bulletin 0580, Section IV.A.3 requires documentation demonstrating that the member has attempted and had an inadequate response to at least **one (1) structured, medically supervised weight loss program** lasting a minimum of **six (6) consecutive months** within the past **24 months**. This program must include dietary modification, behavioral counseling, and increased physical activity, and must be documented in the medical record with visit notes, provider attestation, or program completion records. The submitted documentation does not contain records demonstrating participation in or completion of a qualifying structured weight loss program, nor does it include provider attestation that such a program was attempted and unsuccessful.

**2. Insufficient Laboratory Documentation to Establish Comorbidity Profile**
The request references diagnoses of polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) and insulin resistance as qualifying comorbidities under CPB 0580, Section IV.A.4.
