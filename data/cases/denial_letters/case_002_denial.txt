## REASON FOR DENIAL

UnitedHealthcare's clinical review team, including a licensed physician reviewer board-certified in Internal Medicine, has evaluated the submitted clinical documentation and determined that the request for **tirzepatide (Zepbound)** does not meet the plan's medical necessity criteria for the following reasons:

**1. Incomplete Step Therapy Requirements**

Pursuant to Clinical Policy Bulletin CPB-0580.00, coverage of tirzepatide (Zepbound) for chronic weight management requires documented evidence of an adequate trial and failure of, contraindication to, or intolerance of **at least one prior FDA-approved weight management pharmacotherapy agent** from a different drug class (e.g., phentermine/topiramate [Qsymia], naltrexone/bupropion [Contrave], or orlistat). The clinical documentation submitted does not demonstrate that the member has completed a step therapy trial of a clinically appropriate first-line weight management agent at adequate dosing for a minimum of **twelve (12) consecutive weeks**.
