## POLICY BASIS FOR DENIAL

This determination was made in accordance with:

- **Humana Clinical Policy Bulletin (CPB) No. 0580 – Pharmacologic Management of Obesity and Overweight**, effective date January 1, 2025 (last reviewed March 15, 2025)
- **Humana Pharmacy Coverage and Limitations File – Tier Exception and Step Therapy Protocol, Section 4.3: GLP-1 Receptor Agonists for Weight Management**
- Member's applicable Evidence of Coverage (EOC) document, Section 5.4: Prescription Drug Coverage – Coverage Limitations and Exclusions

Pursuant to CPB No. 0580, liraglutide 3.0 mg (Saxenda®) is considered medically necessary for chronic weight management **only** when **all** of the following criteria are met:

1. The member has an initial Body Mass Index (BMI) **≥ 30 kg/m²**, OR a BMI **≥ 27 kg/m²** in the presence of at least one weight-related comorbidity (e.g., type 2 diabetes mellitus, hypertension, dyslipidemia, obstructive sleep apnea); **AND**
2. The member has documented participation in a **structured, comprehensive behavioral modification and lifestyle intervention program** of at least **six (6) consecutive months** duration within the 24 months prior to the request, with documented evidence of goals, attendance, and outcomes (e.g., dietary counseling, physical activity guidance, behavioral therapy); **AND**
3. The member has demonstrated **insufficient weight loss response** (defined as less than 5% total body weight reduction) during the above qualifying behavioral intervention period; **AND**
4. The prescribing physician has documented that **pharmacologic therapy is appropriate** in the context of the member's overall medical profile, including a review of potential contraindications, drug interactions, and comorbid conditions; **AND**
5. No clinically significant contraindications exist, including but not limited to a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2).
