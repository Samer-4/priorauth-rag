## POLICY BASIS FOR DENIAL

This determination is based on the following:

**Clinical Policy Bulletin: CPB-0580 – Glucagon-Like Peptide-1 (GLP-1) Receptor Agonists for Weight Management and Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus**
*(Effective Date: January 1, 2025 | Last Reviewed: March 15, 2025)*

**Applicable Benefit Provision:** Member's pharmacy benefit plan document, Section 7.4 – Quantity and Duration Limits for Specialty Medications.

Under **CPB-0580** and the member's plan formulary, liraglutide 3 mg (Saxenda®) is subject to a **quantity limit of 3 pens (18 mg/3 mL each) per 30-day supply**, consistent with the FDA-approved dosing titration schedule during the initiation phase. The prescribing provider has requested **5 pens per 30-day supply**, which exceeds the plan-allowed quantity limit by **2 pens (40% above the approved limit)**.

The quantity limit established by Cigna for liraglutide 3 mg (Saxenda®) is based on the following clinical rationale:

1. **FDA-Approved Dosing Titration Schedule:** Per the Saxenda® prescribing information (NDA 206321), liraglutide for chronic weight management is initiated at 0.6 mg subcutaneous injection once daily during Week 1, with weekly dose escalations of 0.6 mg until a maintenance dose of 3.0 mg once daily is reached by Week 5. The quantity limit of 3 pens per 30-day fill is consistent with the maximum consumption rate achievable under the approved titration schedule and maintenance dosing for a 30-day period.
