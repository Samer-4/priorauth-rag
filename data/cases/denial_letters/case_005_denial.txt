### BASIS FOR DENIAL

**Reason for Denial:** An alternative covered drug is available that is clinically appropriate for this member's condition and is covered under the member's current pharmacy benefit plan without the additional requirements applicable to the requested medication.

Pursuant to **Humana Clinical Policy Bulletin CPB-HUM-0580 — Chronic Weight Management: GLP-1 Receptor Agonists and Anti-Obesity Pharmacotherapy**, coverage of **semaglutide 2.4 mg/dose (Wegovy®)** requires demonstration that the member has trialed and had an inadequate response to, or is clinically contraindicated to, at least **one preferred formulary agent** indicated for chronic weight management.

A review of available pharmacy claims data and submitted clinical records for member **Ashley R. Patel (MRN-100238)** does not reflect a documented, adequate trial of **orlistat (Xenical®/Alli®)** or **phentermine/topiramate extended-release (Qsymia®)**, both of which appear on the member's current formulary as **Tier 2 (Preferred Brand) and Tier 3 (Non-Preferred Brand)** alternatives, respectively, for the management of obesity in adult patients with a BMI of ≥30 kg/m² or ≥27 kg/m² with at least one weight-related comorbidity.
