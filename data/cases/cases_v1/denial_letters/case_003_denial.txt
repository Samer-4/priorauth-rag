DETERMINATION

Coverage is not authorized at this time.


BASIS FOR DETERMINATION

Coverage for weight loss medications under this benefit requires documentation of at least six
(6) months of participation in a physician-supervised weight management program before drug
therapy begins. The records submitted do not document six months of participation in such a
program.


CLINICAL REVIEW SUMMARY

Coverage program applied: UnitedHealthcare Pharmacy, Clinical Pharmacy Programs — Prior
Authorization/Notification - Plans with Weight Loss/Appetite Suppression Medication Coverage
(Program Number 2026 P 1114-20, effective 5/1/2026). Section 2, Coverage Criteria, A. Initial
Authorization.

Documentation reviewed:
  - Prior authorization request form received July 28, 2026
  - Endocrinology consultation note dated July 21, 2026, Northgate Endocrine & Metabolic Clinic

The submitted records establish a body mass index of 33.7 kg/m² and a request for the treatment
of obesity. Those elements are consistent with the criteria. The member's age is not at issue.

The submitted records describe nutrition counseling beginning May 5, 2026 and a structured
exercise regimen beginning June 16, 2026. This represents approximately eleven weeks of
documented participation through the date of the most recent submitted note. Because this falls
short of the six-month participation requirement described above, the request is not
authorized.

Approval may be reconsidered upon submission of documentation showing that the required
six-month period of supervised weight management has been completed.


RECONSIDERATION AND APPEAL

determination under the terms of your benefit plan. You may submit additional information in
support of the request, including records from a weight management program, a registered
dietitian, or a behavioral health provider.

For the appeal procedures and timeframes that apply to your plan, or for questions about this
notice, please contact the number for Member Services printed on your health plan ID card.
