DETERMINATION

Coverage is not authorized.


BASIS FOR DETERMINATION

The reauthorization criteria set weight-loss thresholds for specific named products. No
reauthorization threshold is specified for Zepbound. Because the criteria do not provide a
reauthorization pathway for this product, continued coverage cannot be approved.


CLINICAL REVIEW SUMMARY

Coverage program applied: UnitedHealthcare Pharmacy, Clinical Pharmacy Programs — Prior
Authorization/Notification - Plans with Weight Loss/Appetite Suppression Medication Coverage
(Program Number 2026 P 1114-20, effective 5/1/2026). Section 2, Coverage Criteria,
B. Reauthorization.

The reauthorization response criterion is expressed as a set of alternatives keyed to particular
products. It sets a threshold of 3 percent for Qsymia, 4 percent for Saxenda, and for Wegovy the
requirement that "For Wegovy injection or tablet, weight loss of ≥ 5% of baseline body weight".
Zepbound is not named in any of these.

Documentation reviewed:
  - Reauthorization request form received June 8, 2026
  - Internal medicine office note dated June 1, 2026, Halverton Internal Medicine

We note that continuation of lifestyle modification is documented, and that the submitted note
records a baseline weight of 209 pounds on November 24, 2025 and a current weight of 196 pounds.
Those matters are not the basis of this determination.

Because no product-specific reauthorization threshold is stated for Zepbound, the criterion
cannot be met and the request is not authorized.
