Dear Mr. Baptiste:

We have completed our review of the request to continue coverage of your medication.

  Drug requested:          Zepbound (tirzepatide) injection
  Requested dosing:        10 mg subcutaneously once weekly
  Requested indication:    Chronic weight management, continuation of therapy
  Requested duration:      12 months


DETERMINATION

The request is not approved.


REASON FOR DENIAL

The submitted documentation does not establish that you have lost at least 5 percent of your
baseline body weight, or that you have maintained an initial 5 percent weight loss.


POLICY BASIS AND CLINICAL RATIONALE

Applicable coverage policy: Aetna Pharmacy Clinical Policy Bulletin, "Weight Loss (BMI 35)
GIP-GLP-1 GLP-1 Agonists PA with Limit 6450-C UDR 05-2024," CONTINUATION OF THERAPY — Chronic
Weight Management (Adult).

For a Zepbound continuation request, that section requires that all listed criteria be met,
including that "The patient has lost at least 5 percent of baseline body weight OR the patient
has continued to maintain their initial 5 percent weight loss [ACTION REQUIRED: Documentation
is required for approval.]" The section also requires that the patient had a baseline BMI of 35
kg/m² or greater at the start of drug therapy for weight loss, and that at least 3 months of
therapy at a stable maintenance dose has been completed.

Materials reviewed:
  1. Continuation request form received July 20, 2026
  2. Endocrinology office note dated July 13, 2026, Cannonbridge Endocrine Associates

The submitted note records a current weight of 241 pounds at a height of 5 feet 10 inches. The
duration and dosing criteria appear satisfied on the submitted record. However, the note does
not state the percentage of weight lost from baseline, and does not identify which recorded
weight represents the baseline at the start of drug therapy. Without a stated baseline and a
stated percentage reduction, we are unable to confirm that the 5 percent threshold has been
reached. Coverage cannot be authorized on the documentation provided.
