DETERMINATION

Not approved.


WHY THIS REQUEST WAS NOT APPROVED

Coverage of Zepbound for obstructive sleep apnea requires a baseline body mass index of 30 kg/m²
or greater and an apnea-hypopnea index of 15 or more events per hour. Neither threshold is met
on the submitted record.


COVERAGE POLICY AND REVIEW FINDINGS

Policy applied: Cigna National Formulary Coverage Policy, "Weight Loss – Glucagon-Like Peptide-1
Agonists Prior Authorization Policy," section V. Zepbound, 2. Obstructive Sleep Apnea, Moderate
to Severe, in a Patient with Obesity, A) Initial Therapy.

That section requires all of the listed criteria, including that at baseline the patient had a
BMI of 30 kg/m² or greater, and that the patient "has had a sleep study that shows BOTH of the
following": a diagnosis of moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnea, and an apnea-hypopnea
index of 15 or more events per hour. The policy notes that a diagnosis of moderate obstructive
sleep apnea is an apnea-hypopnea index of 15 or more events per hour.

Records reviewed: prior authorization request form dated 05/11/2026; pulmonary and sleep
medicine office note dated 05/04/2026; home sleep apnea test report dated 03/2026.

The sleep study reports an apnea-hypopnea index of 14.6 events per hour, which is below the
threshold of 15 and is characterised in the report itself as mild obstructive sleep apnea. The
recorded body mass index is 28.4 kg/m², with the highest value in the submitted record being
29.0 kg/m². Both thresholds for this indication are therefore unmet.

We also considered the request under section V. Zepbound, 1. Weight Loss in an Adult with
Overweight or Obesity, A) Initial Therapy. That section requires that the patient has engaged in
a trial of behavioral modification and dietary restriction for at least 3 months. The submitted
record documents dietary counselling beginning 03/30/2026, approximately six weeks before this
request. That criterion is not met either.
