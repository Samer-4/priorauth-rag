DETERMINATION

The request is not approved.


REASON FOR DENIAL

For a pediatric Saxenda request, coverage requires a documented body weight above 60 kg. The
records submitted document a body weight of 58.1 kg.


POLICY BASIS AND CLINICAL RATIONALE

Applicable coverage policy: Aetna Pharmacy Clinical Policy Bulletin, "Weight Loss (BMI 35)
GIP-GLP-1 GLP-1 Agonists PA with Limit 6450-C UDR 05-2024," COVERAGE CRITERIA — Chronic Weight
Management (Pediatric) – Saxenda and Wegovy ONLY.

Where the request is for Saxenda, that section requires that all of the listed criteria are met,
including that "The patient has a body weight above 60 kg [ACTION REQUIRED: Documentation is
required for approval.]" and that the patient has a baseline body mass index corresponding to 30
kg/m² or greater for adults by international cut-off points based on the Cole Criteria.

Materials reviewed:
  1. Prior authorization request form received June 15, 2026
  2. Pediatric endocrinology note dated June 8, 2026, Fielding Children's Specialty Center
  3. Nutrition and family programme records, Fielding Children's Specialty Center

We confirm from the submitted records that the patient is 12 years of age, that he participated
in a comprehensive weight management programme with continuing follow-up from November 2025
through May 2026, and that the prescriber has documented a baseline body mass index
corresponding to an adult value of 30 kg/m² or greater by international cut-off points. Those
criteria are met and are not the reason for this decision.

The submitted records document weights of 59.4 kg on December 8, 2025, 58.5 kg on March 9, 2026
and 58.1 kg on June 8, 2026. No recorded weight in the submitted record is above 60 kg. The
weight criterion for a pediatric Saxenda request is therefore not met.
