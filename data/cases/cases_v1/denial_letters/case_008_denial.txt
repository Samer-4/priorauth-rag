DETERMINATION

The request is not approved.


REASON FOR DENIAL

Continuation of therapy requires a weight loss of at least 5 percent of baseline body weight, or
maintenance of an initial 5 percent weight loss. Your documented weight loss is 4.1 percent,
which does not reach the required threshold.


POLICY BASIS AND CLINICAL RATIONALE

Applicable coverage policy: Aetna Pharmacy Clinical Policy Bulletin, "Weight Loss (BMI 35)
GIP-GLP-1 GLP-1 Agonists PA with Limit 6450-C UDR 05-2024," CONTINUATION OF THERAPY — Chronic
Weight Management (Adult).

That section requires that all listed criteria be met, including that the patient has lost at
least 5 percent of baseline body weight, or has continued to maintain an initial 5 percent
weight loss, with documentation required for approval.

Materials reviewed:
  1. Continuation request form received July 6, 2026
  2. Office visit note dated June 29, 2026, Wynnhaven Medical Group

The submitted note documents a baseline weight of 219 pounds on February 16, 2026, the date
liraglutide was started, at a height of 5 feet 6 inches, corresponding to a body mass index of
35.3 kg/m². The baseline BMI criterion is met. Therapy has been continuous since February 16,
2026, a duration of approximately 19 weeks, so the duration criterion is also met.

The current recorded weight is 210 pounds. This is a reduction of 9 pounds, or 4.1 percent of
baseline. Because 4.1 percent is below the 5 percent threshold, the response criterion is not
met and continuation cannot be authorized.
