DETERMINATION

The request is not approved.


REASON FOR DENIAL

Coverage of this medication for chronic weight management requires documented trial and failure
of at least two preferred anti-obesity agents, specifically phentermine and naltrexone/bupropion
(Contrave), prior to approval of a GLP-1 receptor agonist. The submitted records do not document
a trial of either agent.


POLICY BASIS AND CLINICAL RATIONALE

Applicable coverage policy: Aetna Pharmacy Clinical Policy Bulletin, "Weight Loss (BMI 35)
GIP-GLP-1 GLP-1 Agonists PA with Limit 6450-C UDR 05-2024," COVERAGE CRITERIA — Chronic Weight
Management (Adult).

Materials reviewed:
  1. Prior authorization request form received May 26, 2026
  2. Office visit note dated May 18, 2026, Ridgefield Family Practice
  3. Nutrition and behavioral program records, Elmcrest Health Partners

We confirm from the submitted records that you are 55 years of age, that your body mass index is
36.4 kg/m², and that you participated in a comprehensive weight management program with
continuing follow-up from September 2025 through April 2026. Those criteria are met and are not
the reason for this decision.

The submitted records contain no evidence of a trial of phentermine, and no evidence of a trial
of naltrexone/bupropion. The medication history states that no prescription weight-loss
medication has been used. Because the required prior agents have not been trialled, step therapy
is not satisfied and the request is denied.

Approval may be reconsidered on submission of records documenting an adequate trial of each
required agent, or documentation of a clinical contraindication to each.
