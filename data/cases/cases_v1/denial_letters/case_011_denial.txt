DETERMINATION

The request is not approved. This determination rests on two separate grounds, set out below.


GROUND 1 — QUANTITY REQUESTED EXCEEDS THE APPLICABLE LIMIT

The quantity requested exceeds the quantity limit that applies to this strength.

Applicable coverage policy: Aetna Pharmacy Clinical Policy Bulletin, "Weight Loss (BMI 35)
GIP-GLP-1 GLP-1 Agonists PA with Limit 6450-C UDR 05-2024," QUANTITY LIMITS APPLY.

For Wegovy 1.7 mg and 2.4 mg per 0.75 mL, the policy sets a 1-month limit of 3 mL, being one
package of four pens, per 21 days, and a 3-month limit of 9 mL, being three packages of four
pens, per 63 days. The policy notes that the 21-day and 63-day durations are used for 28-day and
84-day fill periods respectively to allow time for refill processing.

The submitted request is for four packages, 12 mL, per 90-day fill. This exceeds the 3-month
limit of three packages, 9 mL.


GROUND 2 — CONTINUATION RESPONSE NOT ESTABLISHED

The submitted documentation does not establish that you have lost at least 5 percent of your
baseline body weight, or that you have maintained an initial 5 percent weight loss.

Applicable section: CONTINUATION OF THERAPY — Chronic Weight Management (Adult). For a Wegovy
continuation request, that section requires that "The patient has lost at least 5 percent of
baseline body weight OR the patient has continued to maintain their initial 5 percent weight
loss [ACTION REQUIRED: Documentation is required for approval.]"

Materials reviewed:
  1. Continuation request form received July 13, 2026
  2. Office visit note dated July 6, 2026, Kettering Family Health Center

The submitted note records a current weight of 199 pounds at a height of 5 feet 4 inches. The
note does not state a percentage reduction from baseline and does not identify which recorded
weight represents the baseline at the start of drug therapy. On the documentation provided we
are unable to confirm that the 5 percent threshold has been reached.

The requirement for at least 3 months of therapy at a stable maintenance dose, and the
requirement for a baseline BMI of 35 kg/m² or greater at the start of drug therapy, both appear
satisfied on the submitted record and are not in dispute.
