DETERMINATION

The request is not approved. Coverage of Wegovy under this pharmacy benefit requires that
specific clinical criteria be met, and the information submitted does not establish that all
of those criteria have been met.

This is not a determination that Wegovy is inappropriate for you. It is a determination that
the documentation we received does not show that one of the coverage requirements has been
satisfied.


REASON FOR DENIAL

The submitted documentation does not establish participation in a comprehensive weight
management program — one encouraging behavioral modification, a reduced calorie diet, and
increased physical activity, with continuing follow-up — for at least 6 months prior to
beginning drug therapy.


POLICY BASIS AND CLINICAL RATIONALE

Applicable coverage policy: Aetna Pharmacy Clinical Policy Bulletin, "Weight Loss (BMI 35)
GIP-GLP-1 GLP-1 Agonists PA with Limit 6450-C UDR 05-2024," COVERAGE CRITERIA — Chronic Weight
Management (Adult).

Under that section, authorization for an adult chronic weight management request requires that
all listed criteria be met, including that the patient "has participated in a comprehensive
weight management program that encourages behavioral modification, reduced calorie diet, and
increased physical activity with continuing follow-up for at least 6 months prior to using drug
therapy." The policy also states that documentation is required for approval.

Materials reviewed for this determination consisted of:

  1. Prior authorization request form completed by Dr. Whitfield, received June 4, 2026
  2. A one-page office visit summary dated June 1, 2026

The request form records a height of 5 feet 5 inches, a weight of 219 pounds, and a body mass
index of 36.4 kg/m². This meets the policy's baseline BMI requirement of 35 kg/m² or greater,
which is therefore not in dispute. The member's age is likewise not in dispute.

Neither submitted document identifies a weight management program, names a program provider,
or records dates of participation or follow-up contacts. The June 1, 2026 visit summary states
only that the member has "been working on diet and exercise." Without dates or a record of
continuing follow-up, we are unable to determine that participation of at least 6 months
occurred prior to the requested start of drug therapy. On that basis the request is denied.


YOUR RIGHT TO APPEAL

You or your authorized representative — including your prescribing provider acting on your

documenting the dates and content of your participation and follow-up.
