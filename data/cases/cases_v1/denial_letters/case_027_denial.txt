DETERMINATION

Coverage is not authorized.


BASIS FOR DETERMINATION

Your body mass index is below 30 kg/m². At a body mass index of 27 kg/m² or greater, coverage
additionally requires a weight-related comorbidity. No weight-related comorbidity is documented.


CLINICAL REVIEW SUMMARY

Coverage program applied: UnitedHealthcare Pharmacy, Clinical Pharmacy Programs — Prior
Authorization/Notification - Plans with Weight Loss/Appetite Suppression Medication Coverage
(Program Number 2026 P 1114-20, effective 5/1/2026). Section 2, Coverage Criteria, A. Initial
Authorization.

That section requires one of the following: a "Body Mass Index (BMI) ≥ 30 kg/m2 or for pediatric
patients a BMI > 95th percentile"; or both a BMI of 27 kg/m² or greater and that the "Patient has
a weight-related comorbidity (e.g., dyslipidemia, hypertension, type 2 diabetes, sleep apnea)".

Documentation reviewed:
  - Prior authorization request form received March 30, 2026
  - Family medicine office note dated March 23, 2026, Kingsholm Family Medicine

The submitted note documents a height of 6 feet 0 inches and weights of 210 pounds on
January 12, 2026 and 206 pounds on March 23, 2026, giving body mass index values of 28.5 and 27.9
kg/m². Neither reaches 30 kg/m², so the first route is unavailable. The second route is available
in principle because both values exceed 27 kg/m².

The submitted note records a family history of type 2 diabetes in the member's father and sister.
A family history is a risk factor rather than a condition the member has. The note records
hemoglobin A1c 5.3 percent, a normal lipid panel, normal blood pressure readings, and no
diagnosis of sleep apnea. No weight-related comorbidity is documented in the member himself.

The age requirement, the covered indication and use as an adjunct to lifestyle modification are
satisfied and are not at issue.
