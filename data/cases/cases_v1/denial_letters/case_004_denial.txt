DETERMINATION

The request is not approved.


REASON FOR DENIAL

The documented body mass index does not meet the minimum required for coverage of this
medication for chronic weight management.


POLICY BASIS AND CLINICAL RATIONALE

Applicable coverage policy: Aetna Pharmacy Clinical Policy Bulletin, "Weight Loss (BMI 35)
GIP-GLP-1 GLP-1 Agonists PA with Limit 6450-C UDR 05-2024," COVERAGE CRITERIA — Chronic Weight
Management (Adult).

That section requires that all listed criteria be met, including that "The patient has a
baseline body mass index (BMI) greater than or equal to 35 kg/m2 [ACTION REQUIRED:
Documentation is required for approval.]"

Materials reviewed:
  1. Prior authorization request form received March 10, 2026
  2. Office visit note dated March 2, 2026, Brookmere Internal Medicine
  3. Nutrition program summary, Fairhaven Weight Management Services

We acknowledge the documentation of your participation in a weight management program. The
program criterion is met and is not the reason for this decision. Your age also is not at
issue.

The submitted records document a height of 5 feet 7 inches with weights ranging from 219 to 222
pounds between August 2025 and March 2026, corresponding to body mass index values from 34.3 to
34.8 kg/m². The most recent recorded value is 34.6 kg/m². No recorded value in the submitted
record reaches 35 kg/m². The BMI criterion is therefore not met and coverage cannot be
authorized.
