DETERMINATION

Not approved.


WHY THIS REQUEST WAS NOT APPROVED

The member's baseline body mass index is below 30 kg/m². Coverage at a body mass index of 27
kg/m² or greater requires at least one weight-related comorbidity from the list in the policy.
No qualifying comorbidity was identified in the submitted records.


COVERAGE POLICY AND REVIEW FINDINGS

Policy applied: Cigna National Formulary Coverage Policy, "Weight Loss – Glucagon-Like Peptide-1
Agonists Prior Authorization Policy," section III. Wegovy injection and Wegovy HD injection,
1. Weight Loss in an Adult with Overweight or Obesity, A) Initial Therapy.

That section requires the patient to meet one of two body mass index routes: a baseline BMI of 30
kg/m² or greater, or a baseline BMI of 27 kg/m² or greater together with at least one of the
weight-related comorbidities the policy lists.

Records reviewed: prior authorization request form dated 06/01/2026; obstetrics and gynecology
office note dated 05/25/2026, Caldermill Women's Health.

The submitted records document a height of 5 feet 6 inches and weights between 174 and 179
pounds, giving body mass index values between 28.1 and 28.9 kg/m². The first route is therefore
unavailable. Age and the three-month behavioral and dietary trial are satisfied and are not at
issue.

The submitted note is a lengthy gynaecological consultation addressing several matters. Our
reviewer identified anaemia, migraine, acne, anxiety and plantar fasciitis. None of these appears
on the policy's list of weight-related comorbidities. On that basis the second route is not
established and the request is denied.
