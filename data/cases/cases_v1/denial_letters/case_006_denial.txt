DETERMINATION

The request is not approved.


REASON FOR DENIAL

The cardiovascular risk-reduction criteria for this medication exclude members who have type 2
diabetes. Your records document type 2 diabetes. The alternative coverage pathway, chronic
weight management, requires a body mass index of at least 35 kg/m², which your records do not
document.


POLICY BASIS AND CLINICAL RATIONALE

Applicable coverage policy: Aetna Pharmacy Clinical Policy Bulletin, "Weight Loss (BMI 35)
GIP-GLP-1 GLP-1 Agonists PA with Limit 6450-C UDR 05-2024."

Under COVERAGE CRITERIA — Risk Reduction of Major Adverse Cardiovascular Events (Adult) —
Wegovy ONLY, authorization requires that all listed criteria be met, including that "The patient
does NOT have type 2 diabetes." The policy carries a note that Ozempic is indicated to reduce
the risk of major cardiovascular events in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus and established
cardiovascular disease, and that patients with type 2 diabetes may be treated for cardiovascular
risk reduction with Ozempic.

Under COVERAGE CRITERIA — Chronic Weight Management (Adult), authorization requires among other
criteria that "The patient has a baseline body mass index (BMI) greater than or equal to 35
kg/m2 [ACTION REQUIRED: Documentation is required for approval.]"

Materials reviewed:
  1. Prior authorization request form received February 2, 2026
  2. Cardiology office note dated January 26, 2026, Lakeshore Cardiovascular Institute
  3. Laboratory results dated January 12, 2026

We confirm from the submitted records that you have established cardiovascular disease with a
documented myocardial infarction in March 2024, that you are receiving guideline-directed
medical therapy, and that your body mass index of 31.7 kg/m² exceeds the 27 kg/m² threshold that
applies within the cardiovascular pathway. Those elements are met.

However, the submitted records also document type 2 diabetes, with a hemoglobin A1c of 7.1
percent and ongoing treatment with metformin and empagliflozin. The cardiovascular pathway
therefore cannot be used.

Reviewing the request against the chronic weight management pathway instead, the highest body
mass index recorded in the submitted records is 32.6 kg/m², below the required 35 kg/m².

Neither pathway is satisfied and the request is denied.
