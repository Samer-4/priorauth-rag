DETERMINATION

The request is not approved.


REASON FOR DENIAL

The policy requires documentation of a baseline body mass index of 35 kg/m² or greater. The
submitted records do not provide the documentation required to establish the baseline body mass
index.


POLICY BASIS AND CLINICAL RATIONALE

Applicable coverage policy: Aetna Pharmacy Clinical Policy Bulletin, "Weight Loss (BMI 35)
GIP-GLP-1 GLP-1 Agonists PA with Limit 6450-C UDR 05-2024," COVERAGE CRITERIA — Chronic Weight
Management (Adult).

That section requires among other criteria that "The patient has a baseline body mass index
(BMI) greater than or equal to 35 kg/m2 [ACTION REQUIRED: Documentation is required for
approval.]"

Materials reviewed:
  1. Prior authorization request form received March 3, 2026
  2. Office visit note dated February 24, 2026, Halloway Rheumatology and Internal Medicine

The prior authorization form was submitted with the height and weight fields left blank and the
BMI field marked "see notes." The accompanying office note is a lengthy multi-problem
consultation. Our reviewer was unable to identify documentation establishing the baseline body
mass index within the submitted material. The policy states that documentation is required for
approval of this criterion.

The weight management program criterion appears satisfied on the submitted record and is not the
reason for this decision. Your age is likewise not at issue.

Approval may be reconsidered on submission of documentation of height, weight and body mass
index.
