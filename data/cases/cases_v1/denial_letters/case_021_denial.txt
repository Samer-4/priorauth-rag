WHY THIS REQUEST WAS NOT APPROVED

The cardiovascular pathway requires a prior myocardial infarction, a prior stroke, or
symptomatic peripheral arterial disease. The cerebrovascular event documented in the record is a
transient ischemic attack, which the policy expressly excludes.


COVERAGE POLICY AND REVIEW FINDINGS

Policy applied: Cigna National Formulary Coverage Policy, "Weight Loss – Glucagon-Like Peptide-1
Agonists Prior Authorization Policy," section III. Wegovy injection and Wegovy HD injection,
3. Major Adverse Cardiovascular Event(s) Risk Reduction in a Patient with Established
Cardiovascular Disease with Overweight or Obesity.

That section requires the patient to meet one of three qualifying events: a prior myocardial
infarction; a prior stroke, with the note that "This does not include a transient ischemic
attack (TIA)."; or a history of symptomatic peripheral arterial disease evidenced by intermittent
claudication with an ankle-brachial index below 0.85, a peripheral arterial revascularization
procedure, or amputation due to atherosclerotic disease.

Records reviewed: prior authorization request form dated 02/16/2026; family medicine office note
dated 02/09/2026, Harrowfield Family Medicine; neurology consultation letter dated 07/2024.

The member's body mass index of 29.1 kg/m² satisfies the 27 kg/m² threshold for this pathway, and
he is on cardiovascular pharmacotherapy. Those elements are met.

The problem list in the family medicine note carries "CVA 2024." The neurology consultation
letter of 07/2024, which is the source document for that entry, records a transient ischemic
attack: right arm weakness and dysarthria with complete resolution within 40 minutes, and
magnetic resonance imaging with diffusion-weighted sequences showing no acute infarction. The
diagnosis recorded by the neurologist is transient ischemic attack. No myocardial infarction and
no peripheral arterial disease is documented.

We also considered the request under section III, 1. Weight Loss in an Adult with Overweight or
Obesity, A) Initial Therapy. That section requires a trial of behavioral modification and
dietary restriction for at least 3 months. The record documents dietary counselling beginning
01/19/2026, approximately four weeks before this request. That criterion is not met either.
