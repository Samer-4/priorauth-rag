DETERMINATION

Coverage is not authorized.


BASIS FOR DETERMINATION

Your plan is a North Dakota fully insured Essential Health Benefits individual market plan. The
coverage criteria that apply to that plan require a body mass index of 40 kg/m² or greater. Your
documented body mass index is 36.3 kg/m².


CLINICAL REVIEW SUMMARY

Coverage program applied: UnitedHealthcare Pharmacy, Clinical Pharmacy Programs — Prior
Authorization/Notification - Plans with Weight Loss/Appetite Suppression Medication Coverage
(Program Number 2026 P 1114-20, effective 5/1/2026), Section 3, Coverage Criteria for North
Dakota Fully Insured EHB (Small Group and Individual) Plans, A. Initial Authorization.

Your plan falls within Section 3. Where Section 3 applies, it governs in place of the general
criteria at Section 2.

Section 3 requires one of the following: a "Body Mass Index (BMI) ≥ 40 kg/m2 or for pediatric
patients a BMI > 120% of 95th percentile"; or that Wegovy injection is being requested for the
treatment of metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis.

Documentation reviewed:
  - Prior authorization request form received April 27, 2026
  - Internal medicine office note dated April 20, 2026, Red River Internal Medicine, Fargo,
    North Dakota

The submitted note documents a height of 5 feet 5 inches and weights of 223 pounds on January 26,
2026 and 218 pounds on April 20, 2026, giving body mass index values of 37.1 and 36.3 kg/m².
Neither reaches 40 kg/m². The request is for weight loss and not for metabolic
dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis, so the alternative route in Section 3 is not available.

The indication, the age requirement and use as an adjunct to lifestyle modification are all
satisfied under Section 3 and are not at issue.

We note that the body mass index threshold in the general criteria at Section 2 is 30 kg/m². That
section does not apply to this plan.
