DETERMINATION

Coverage is not authorized at this time.


BASIS FOR DETERMINATION

Initial authorization requires a body mass index of 30 kg/m² or greater, or a body mass index of
27 kg/m² or greater with a weight-related comorbidity. The submitted materials do not document a
body mass index.


CLINICAL REVIEW SUMMARY

Coverage program applied: UnitedHealthcare Pharmacy, Clinical Pharmacy Programs — Prior
Authorization/Notification - Plans with Weight Loss/Appetite Suppression Medication Coverage
(Program Number 2026 P 1114-20, effective 5/1/2026). Section 2, Coverage Criteria, A. Initial
Authorization.

That section requires one of the following: "Body Mass Index (BMI) ≥ 30 kg/m2 or for pediatric
patients a BMI > 95th percentile", or a BMI of 27 kg/m² or greater together with a weight-related
comorbidity.

Documentation reviewed:
  - Prior authorization request form received June 15, 2026

The request form was submitted on its own. The height, weight and body mass index fields on the
form are blank. No clinical records accompanied the request. The remaining criteria — a covered
indication, the age requirement, and use as an adjunct to lifestyle modification — are
addressed on the form and are not at issue.

Approval may be reconsidered on submission of clinical records documenting height, weight and
body mass index.
