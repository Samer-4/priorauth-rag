WHY THIS REQUEST WAS NOT APPROVED

Continuation of coverage requires documentation that the patient has lost at least 5 percent of
baseline body weight. The submission did not include documentation of weight loss.


COVERAGE POLICY AND REVIEW FINDINGS

Policy applied: Cigna National Formulary Coverage Policy, "Weight Loss – Glucagon-Like Peptide-1
Agonists Prior Authorization Policy," section III. Wegovy injection and Wegovy HD injection,
1. Weight Loss in an Adult with Overweight or Obesity, B) Patient is Currently Receiving Wegovy
injection, Wegovy HD injection, or Wegovy tablet.

That section requires all of the listed criteria, including that "Patient has lost ≥ 5% of
baseline body weight," where baseline refers to baseline prior to any GLP-1 agonist or GLP-1/GIP
receptor agonist.

Records reviewed: continuation request form dated 06/08/2026.

The continuation request form was submitted without any accompanying clinical documentation. The
form records the member's current weight but no baseline weight and no percentage reduction. On
the material submitted we cannot confirm that the response criterion is met.

Approval may be reconsidered on submission of clinical records documenting the baseline weight
and the current weight, or the percentage reduction.
