DETERMINATION

Not approved.


WHY THIS REQUEST WAS NOT APPROVED

The request is for Zepbound to be added to the member's existing Ozempic therapy. Concomitant use
of these agents is not covered under this policy.


COVERAGE POLICY AND REVIEW FINDINGS

Policy applied: Cigna National Formulary Coverage Policy, "Weight Loss – Glucagon-Like Peptide-1
Agonists Prior Authorization Policy," CONDITIONS NOT COVERED, 1. Concomitant Use with
Glucagon-Like Peptide-1 (GLP-1) Agonists or GLP-1/Glucose-Dependent Insulinotropic Polypeptide
(GIP) Agonists.

That section provides that "The GLP-1 agonists and the GLP-1/GIP agonist should not be combined
with each other or with any other GLP-1 agonists or GLP-1/GIP agonist." The policy names Ozempic
among its examples of other GLP-1 agonists.

Records reviewed: prior authorization request form dated 03/16/2026; endocrinology office note
dated 03/09/2026, Wexford Endocrinology.

The submitted note records that the member has been taking Ozempic 1 mg weekly since 2024 for
type 2 diabetes, that this therapy is to continue, and that Zepbound is requested in addition
for weight management. Zepbound is a GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist and Ozempic is a GLP-1 agonist.
Their concomitant use falls squarely within the excluded use above.

We note that the remaining criteria under section V. Zepbound, 1. Weight Loss in an Adult with
Overweight or Obesity, A) Initial Therapy — age, the three-month behavioral and dietary trial,
the baseline body mass index, and concomitant lifestyle measures — appear satisfied on the
submitted record. The exclusion is nonetheless determinative.
