Agonists Prior Authorization Policy," section V. Zepbound, 1. Weight Loss in an Adult with
Overweight or Obesity, A) Initial Therapy.

That section requires, among other criteria, that the "Patient has engaged in a trial of
behavioral modification and dietary restriction for at least 3 months."

Records reviewed: prior authorization request form dated 04/06/2026; internal medicine office
note dated 04/06/2026, Bellmount Internal Medicine.

The submitted note establishes a body mass index of 33.1 kg/m², which satisfies the baseline
requirement, and the member's age and concomitant lifestyle measures are also satisfied. None of

before this request. It does not describe any earlier programme. On the record submitted, the
three-month criterion is not met.

Approval may be reconsidered on submission of records documenting a qualifying trial not
