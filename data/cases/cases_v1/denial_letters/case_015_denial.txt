WHY THIS REQUEST WAS NOT APPROVED

Continuation of coverage requires that the patient has lost at least 5 percent of baseline body
weight. The documented reduction is 4.3 percent.


COVERAGE POLICY AND REVIEW FINDINGS

Policy applied: Cigna National Formulary Coverage Policy, "Weight Loss – Glucagon-Like Peptide-1
Agonists Prior Authorization Policy," section I. Liraglutide (Saxenda, generic), 1. Weight Loss
in an Adult with Overweight or Obesity, B) Patient is Currently Receiving liraglutide.

Continuation under this policy requires that the patient has lost at least 5 percent of baseline
body weight, where baseline refers to baseline prior to any GLP-1 agonist or GLP-1/GIP receptor
agonist.

Records reviewed: continuation request form dated 06/29/2026; internal medicine office note
dated 06/22/2026, Ashcombe Internal Medicine.

The submitted note documents a pre-treatment weight of 233 pounds on 10/06/2025 at a height of 5
feet 8 inches, giving a baseline body mass index of 35.4 kg/m². The baseline BMI criterion is
met. The member's age and the concomitant behavioral modification and reduced-calorie diet
criteria are also met and are not at issue.

The current recorded weight is 223 pounds, a reduction of 10 pounds. That is 4.3 percent of
baseline, which is below the required 5 percent. The response criterion is not met and
continuation cannot be approved.
