DETERMINATION

BASIS FOR DETERMINATION

Reauthorization requires a weight loss of 5 percent or more of baseline body weight. The
documented reduction is 4.9 percent.


CLINICAL REVIEW SUMMARY

Coverage program applied: UnitedHealthcare Pharmacy, Clinical Pharmacy Programs — Prior
Authorization/Notification - Plans with Weight Loss/Appetite Suppression Medication Coverage
(Program Number 2026 P 1114-20, effective 5/1/2026). Section 2, Coverage Criteria,
B. Reauthorization.

That section requires, for Wegovy injection or tablet, a "weight loss of ≥ 5% of baseline body
weight", together with continuation of lifestyle modification.

Documentation reviewed:
  - Reauthorization request form received June 1, 2026
  - Family medicine office note dated May 25, 2026, Wrenfield Family Medicine

The submitted note records a baseline weight of 217 pounds on December 15, 2025, the date therapy
began, and a current weight of 206 pounds on May 25, 2026. That is a reduction of 11 pounds. Our
calculation of that reduction is 4.9 percent of baseline, which is below the required 5 percent.

Continuation of lifestyle modification is documented and is not at issue.
