DETERMINATION

Not approved.


WHY THIS REQUEST WAS NOT APPROVED

The documented body mass index is 28.7 kg/m², which is below the 30 kg/m² threshold, and no
weight-related comorbidity was identified that would permit approval at a body mass index of 27
kg/m² or greater.


COVERAGE POLICY AND REVIEW FINDINGS

Policy applied: Cigna National Formulary Coverage Policy, "Weight Loss – Glucagon-Like Peptide-1
Agonists Prior Authorization Policy," section III. Wegovy injection and Wegovy HD injection,
1. Weight Loss in an Adult with Overweight or Obesity, A) Initial Therapy.

That section requires the patient to meet one of two body mass index routes: at baseline a BMI of
30 kg/m² or greater, or at baseline a BMI of 27 kg/m² or greater together with at least one
listed weight-related comorbidity.

Records reviewed: prior authorization request form dated 06/22/2026; internal medicine office
note dated 06/15/2026, Aldgate Internal Medicine.

The submitted note records a height of 5 feet 4 inches and a weight of 167 pounds, giving a body
mass index of 28.7 kg/m². The note also records a weight of 172 pounds on 03/02/2026, a body
mass index of 29.5 kg/m². Neither reaches 30 kg/m².

The submitted records do not identify a weight-related comorbidity from the policy's list. Age
and the three-month behavioral and dietary trial are satisfied and are not at issue.

Because neither body mass index route is established on the submitted record, the request is
denied.
