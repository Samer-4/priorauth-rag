DETERMINATION

Coverage is not authorized at this time.


BASIS FOR DETERMINATION

Coverage of Imcivree requires that the patient is currently enrolled in, or has a history of, a
weight loss management program. The submitted records document neither.


CLINICAL REVIEW SUMMARY

Coverage program applied: UnitedHealthcare Pharmacy, Clinical Pharmacy Programs — Prior
Authorization/Notification - Plans with Weight Loss/Appetite Suppression Medication Coverage
(Program Number 2026 P 1114-20, effective 5/1/2026). Section 2, Coverage Criteria, A. Initial
Authorization, criteria for Imcivree.

Those criteria require all of the following: a diagnosis of obesity due to POMC, PCSK1 or LEPR
gene deficiency confirmed by genetic testing, or a diagnosis of Bardet-Biedl syndrome; an adult
body mass index of 30 kg/m² or greater, or for a pediatric patient weight above the 95th
percentile for age; and that the "Patient is currently enrolled in or has history of a weight
loss management program".

Documentation reviewed:
  - Prior authorization request form received February 23, 2026
  - Medical genetics clinic note dated February 16, 2026, Thornhill Genetics Service
  - Molecular genetics report dated 2019

We confirm from the submitted records that Bardet-Biedl syndrome is established, with a
pathogenic BBS1 variant reported on molecular testing and a clinical picture consistent with the
diagnosis. We also confirm a body mass index of 38.4 kg/m². Those two criteria are met and are
not the reason for this decision.

The submitted note records that no dietitian referral has been made, that a referral offered in
2024 was declined, and that the patient has not attended any weight management programme. The
third criterion is therefore not established.

Approval may be reconsidered on submission of documentation of current enrolment in, or a history
of, a weight loss management program.
