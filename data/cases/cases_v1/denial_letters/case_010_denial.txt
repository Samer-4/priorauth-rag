DETERMINATION

The request is not approved.


REASON FOR DENIAL

The submitted documentation does not establish participation in a comprehensive weight
management program with continuing follow-up for at least 6 months prior to beginning drug
therapy.


POLICY BASIS AND CLINICAL RATIONALE

Applicable coverage policy: Aetna Pharmacy Clinical Policy Bulletin, "Weight Loss (BMI 35)
GIP-GLP-1 GLP-1 Agonists PA with Limit 6450-C UDR 05-2024."

The policy requires that "The patient has participated in a comprehensive weight management
program that encourages behavioral modification, reduced calorie diet, and increased physical
activity with continuing follow-up for at least 6 months prior to using drug therapy."

Materials reviewed:
  1. Prior authorization request form received April 13, 2026
  2. Neurology office note dated April 6, 2026, Stonegate Neurology Associates
  3. Cardiac rehabilitation discharge summary dated March 2026

We confirm from the submitted records that you have established cardiovascular disease with a
documented ischemic stroke in November 2024, that your body mass index of 29.4 kg/m² is above
the 27 kg/m² threshold, that you do not have type 2 diabetes, and that you are receiving
guideline-directed medical therapy. Those elements are met.

The submitted records document participation in a cardiac rehabilitation programme from January
to March 2026, a period of approximately ten weeks, together with two dietitian appointments.
This does not amount to six months of participation in a comprehensive weight management
program. On that basis the request is denied.
