DETERMINATION

Not approved.


WHY THIS REQUEST WAS NOT APPROVED

Coverage of Zepbound for weight loss in an adult requires that the patient has engaged in a
trial of behavioral modification and dietary restriction for at least 3 months. The clinical
record submitted does not support that this has occurred.


COVERAGE POLICY AND REVIEW FINDINGS

Policy applied: Cigna National Formulary Coverage Policy, "Weight Loss – Glucagon-Like
Peptide-1 Agonists Prior Authorization Policy," section V. Zepbound, 1. Weight Loss in an Adult
with Overweight or Obesity, A) Initial Therapy.

That section requires, among other criteria, that the "Patient has engaged in a trial of
behavioral modification and dietary restriction for at least 3 months."

Records reviewed: prior authorization request form dated 05/19/2026 and office note from
Harborview Primary Care Associates dated 05/11/2026.

The office note documents a body mass index of 31.5 kg/m², which satisfies the baseline BMI
criterion of 30 kg/m² or greater. Age and the concomitant behavioral modification and
reduced-calorie diet criteria are also satisfied and are not at issue.

The behavioral and dietary trial is at issue. The prior authorization form states that the
patient has completed "6 months of diet and exercise." The clinical record does not support
that statement. The dated entries in the submitted note record the first nutrition visit on
03/16/2026 and a second on 05/04/2026, and the note states that there was no structured
weight-management or nutrition program before March 2026. Measured from the first documented
contact on 03/16/2026 to the date of this request, the documented trial is approximately nine
weeks. Measured to the date of the submitted note, it is eight weeks. Either way it falls short
of three months.

Because the clinical record does not establish a three-month trial, the criterion is not met
and the request cannot be approved.


APPEAL RIGHTS

The member, or the prescriber acting as the member's authorized representative, may request
reconsideration of this determination under the appeal provisions of the applicable benefit
plan. Additional clinical documentation may be submitted with the request. Documentation that
would bear on this determination includes dated records from a dietitian, nutrition program, or
behavioral health provider covering a period of at least three months, or records of an earlier
qualifying trial not previously submitted.

For questions about this notice or the appeal process that applies to this plan, contact
Customer Service using the information on the member identification card.
