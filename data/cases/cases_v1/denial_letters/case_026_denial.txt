DETERMINATION

Coverage is not authorized at this time.


BASIS FOR DETERMINATION

Initial authorization requires that the medication be used as an adjunct to lifestyle
modification. The submitted records do not document lifestyle modification.


CLINICAL REVIEW SUMMARY

Coverage program applied: UnitedHealthcare Pharmacy, Clinical Pharmacy Programs — Prior
Authorization/Notification - Plans with Weight Loss/Appetite Suppression Medication Coverage
(Program Number 2026 P 1114-20, effective 5/1/2026). Section 2, Coverage Criteria, A. Initial
Authorization.

That section requires that the medication be "Used as an adjunct to lifestyle modification (e.g.,
dietary or caloric restriction, exercise, behavioral support, community-based program)".

Documentation reviewed:
  - Prior authorization request form received July 13, 2026
  - Family medicine office note dated July 6, 2026, Marlow Bridge Family Practice

The body mass index of 34.9 kg/m² satisfies the body mass index criterion, the indication is a
covered one, and the age requirement is met. None of those is at issue.

The submitted note does not record a referral to a dietitian, enrolment in a weight management
programme, or a documented dietary or exercise prescription. No structured lifestyle intervention
was identified in the record. On that basis the adjunct criterion is not established.

Approval may be reconsidered on submission of documentation of lifestyle modification.
