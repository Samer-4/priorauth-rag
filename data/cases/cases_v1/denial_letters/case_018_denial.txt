DETERMINATION

Not approved.


WHY THIS REQUEST WAS NOT APPROVED

Coverage of a GLP-1 receptor agonist for weight loss requires documented trial and inadequate
response to two lower-cost anti-obesity agents, orlistat and phentermine, before a GLP-1 agent
is approved. The submitted records document neither trial.


COVERAGE POLICY AND REVIEW FINDINGS

Policy applied: Cigna National Formulary Coverage Policy, "Weight Loss – Glucagon-Like Peptide-1
Agonists Prior Authorization Policy," section III. Wegovy injection and Wegovy HD injection,
1. Weight Loss in an Adult with Overweight or Obesity, A) Initial Therapy.

Records reviewed: prior authorization request form dated 04/20/2026; family medicine office note
dated 04/13/2026, Thornbury Family Medicine; dietitian summary dated 04/2026.

We confirm from the submitted records that the member is 47 years of age, that the baseline body
mass index is 34.1 kg/m² and the current value 33.2 kg/m², and that he has engaged in a trial of
behavioral modification and dietary restriction since 01/05/2026, in excess of three months.
Those criteria are met and are not the reason for this decision.

The medication history in the submitted note records no prior anti-obesity pharmacotherapy of
any kind. Because neither orlistat nor phentermine has been trialled, the step therapy sequence
is not satisfied and the request cannot be approved.

Approval may be reconsidered on submission of records documenting an adequate trial of each
agent, or documentation of intolerance or contraindication to each.
