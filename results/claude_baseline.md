# Claude Baseline — Phase 1

Model: Claude Sonnet 5
Cases: 30

## Results

- Pipeline completed: 11/30 (36.7%)
- Pipeline failed: 19/30 (63.3%)
- Completed with no Stage 6 findings: 1/30
- Completed with Stage 6 findings: 10/30

## Major Failure Pattern

Stage 3 was the dominant failure point.

- 12 Stage 3 evidence verification failures
- 3 Stage 3 structured-output failures
- Additional failures occurred in Stage 2, Stage 5, and Stage 6.

## Notes

This is a single-run baseline.
A completed case does not necessarily mean the generated appeal was correct.
Stage 6 findings represent verifier-detected issues, not ground-truth accuracy.
